TileSense support

For help with TileSense on Android or iOS, email support@ericrxu.com. For a game issue, include your device, the ruleset, and what happened at the table.

To play with friends, choose Play Online, create a room and give your friends the six-character code. Single-player games and the daily quiz work offline.

For information about multiplayer records or deletion requests, read the mobile privacy policy.
