TileSense mobile privacy policy

Eric R. Xu develops TileSense for Android and iOS. This policy covers the mobile apps. Updated November 19, 2026.

Playing on your own

Single-player games, practice hands and daily puzzles run on your device. The mobile app does not send single-player analytics. You can play without an account. The app has no ads, purchases or cross-app tracking.

Joining an online room

When you choose online play, TileSense connects to its multiplayer service over an encrypted connection. The service receives your network address to handle the connection. It uses a random guest identifier, your chosen display name and character, the room code and your moves to seat you, run the game and reconnect you if the connection drops. You can use a nickname. Other players in the room see your display name, character and public play.

The service can record room and session identifiers, seats, moves, settings, scores and results to understand how games are played. These records can be linked to your guest identifier. The mobile app generates a new guest identity on a fresh launch. TileSense uses hosting providers to operate the service and does not sell player data or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Live rooms are held in server memory. The standard retention window for multiplayer analytics is 90 days. Infrastructure logs and backups may have separate retention periods. To ask about or request deletion of your records, email the developer privately with the approximate date and time of play. Include the room code if the game has already ended. Never send passwords or an active room code.

Contact
