TileSense Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads. TileSense does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.
  • The iOS, iPadOS and Android apps are fully offline. Everything runs on your device, and the apps do not send anything to us.
  • Play Online (private rooms with friends) exists only in the web version. It sends the game moves, a random guest ID and the display name you choose to our server so the game can run.
  • The web version also sends privacy-light usage statistics about how the game is played, tied to a random ID, not to you.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

1. Who we are

TileSense is a mahjong game and trainer published by Eric Xu, an individual developer. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean that publisher. You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

This policy covers the TileSense apps for iOS, iPadOS and Android, and the web version at app.ericrxu.com/tilesense.

2. The iOS, iPadOS and Android apps

The mobile apps are fully offline. Single-player games, the Hand Builder, Learn / Train, and the guide that grades your decisions all run entirely on your device. The apps do not connect to our servers, do not need an internet connection to play, and do not collect or transmit any personal information, usage statistics, device identifiers or gameplay data. Play Online and the usage statistics described below are not part of the mobile apps.

The only time the apps reach the internet is if you tap a link (for example, to a rulebook reference or to our source code on GitHub). That opens in your browser, and that site’s own privacy policy applies.

On the web version, small preferences such as your language, whether you have seen a tutorial, and the display name and character you picked for online play are kept in your browser’s local storage. You can erase them by clearing site data for the page.

3. Play Online (web version only)

When you use Play Online in the web version, your browser connects over an encrypted WebSocket connection (wss://) to our game server. To run the game, the server receives:

  • a random guest ID generated by the app, which lets you reconnect to your seat if your connection drops;
  • the display name and character you choose (please do not use your real name or anything personal in your display name);
  • the room code, your seat, and your game actions (such as discards and calls); and
  • your IP address, which any internet connection necessarily reveals to the server. We use it to limit abuse and do not write it to our database or logs in full (see section 5).

Other players in your room can see your display name and character. Display names are held in the room’s memory on our server while the room is active and are not written to our statistics database. Rooms are removed from the server when they are left empty.

For each online match, our statistics database also records the room code, the guest IDs that sat in each seat, the final result, and the game settings, kept as described in section 6. There is no chat feature, and no guide or auto-play is available online.

4. Usage statistics (web version)

The web version sends gameplay statistics to our server by default. The iOS and Android apps do not send these statistics. The statistics include:

WhatWhy
A random client ID (stored in your browser) and a random session IDTo count distinct players and sessions without knowing who you are
App version, and your browser’s user-agent stringTo find bugs on specific browsers and versions
Approximate location (country, region, city) looked up from your IP address, plus the first part of your IP address (the /24 for IPv4 or /48 for IPv6) and a one-way scrambled hash of the full addressTo understand roughly where players are, and to group activity from one network without storing the address itself
Gameplay events: match and round start and end, each discard or call, scores and results, which game settings you used, whether you followed the guide’s suggestion, and your answers to the daily quizTo improve the guide, the bots and the teaching material

We do not store your full IP address, name, email or any account, and we do not use these statistics for advertising. The location lookup uses a local database (“IP Geolocation by DB-IP”), so your IP address is not sent to a third party for it.

5. Third parties and service providers

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. TileSense does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media code.
  • Hosting. Our servers run with a cloud hosting provider, which processes data on our behalf only to host the service. Access logging is turned off for our statistics and online-game endpoints.
  • Links you open. If you tap a link inside the app (for example, to our source code on GitHub), it opens in your browser and that site’s own privacy policy applies.
  • We do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are used in privacy laws such as the CCPA, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

6. How long we keep data

Usage statistics and online-match records are deleted automatically after 90 days by a nightly cleanup job. Data held only in a live online room is discarded when the room closes.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use the iOS or Android app and nothing is sent to us.
  • Reset your web ID by clearing the site’s data in your browser; you will get a new random ID.
  • Ask us to delete or show you your data. We cannot look up data by name because we do not know who you are. The web version shows your client ID in the corner of the screen; email us that ID (and, for online play, your guest ID or room code if you have it) and we will delete the matching records.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us and we will honor those rights.
  • The web version does not yet include an in-app switch to turn off usage statistics. If you would like us to stop collecting them for your client ID, email us.

8. Children

TileSense is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no accounts, and the only free text a player can enter is a display name for online play, which exists only in the web version. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Connections to our servers use encryption in transit (HTTPS and wss://). We keep the data we hold to a minimum, store no passwords or payment details (the app has none), and delete old data automatically. No system is perfectly secure, but collecting little is our main protection.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. For significant changes we will also say so in the app’s release notes.
